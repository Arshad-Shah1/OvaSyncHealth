What is PCOD?

PCOD (Polycystic Ovarian Disease) is a condition where the ovaries produce immature eggs which become cysts.

PCOD Diet Plan

Meal Time Food Options Benefits
Breakfast Oats with chia seeds + boiled egg / vegetable omelette High fiber and protein, helps control blood sugar
Mid-Morning Snack Apple, guava, or a handful of almonds Provides vitamins and healthy fats
Lunch 2 multigrain rotis + dal + mixed vegetables + saladP Balanced meal with protein, fiber, and complex carbs
Evening Snack Roasted chana or sprouts chaat + green tea Keeps energy stable and reduces cravings
Dinner Grilled paneer/tofu + vegetable soup or quinoa Light meal with protein and nutrients
Before Bed Warm turmeric milk (low-fat) or chamomile tea Helps relaxation and supports metabolism
